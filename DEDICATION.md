# In memory of Paul M. Holdren

This project's lung-cancer work is dedicated to my father, **Paul M. Holdren**, whom I lost to lung cancer.

The structures folded here — EGFR with osimertinib, KRASG12C with sotorasib — are among the targets and therapies at the very heart of modern lung-cancer treatment. Predicting them freely, NVIDIA-free on Intel Arc Battlemage hardware, is the very humble beginning of my contributions to the field of medicine. I hope the tools I help assemble, port or even make from scratch can advance scientific progress in drug discovery, genetic markers, biochemistry and beyond.

My work, as lead developer and founder of Universal Basic, is dedicated to the man who had an unshakable belief in me, even when I didn’t believe in myself. Your memory will inspire all of my work, rest easy dad. 🕊
